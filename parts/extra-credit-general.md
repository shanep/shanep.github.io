## Introduction

This is the catch-all for extra credit offered during the semester that does not
have an assignment of its own: something announced in class, a bonus part of a
project, or work you did beyond what was asked.

## Tasks

1. Write a short document that says what you did and when it was offered.
2. Include enough evidence that I can verify it: a link to a commit or
   repository, a screenshot, or the work itself.

## Submission

Submit the document as a PDF to get credit.
