## Instructions

Please complete the course evaluation. I hope that you learned a lot in this
class and enjoyed your time.

## Why are we doing this?

- Course evaluations help me and the computer science department understand
  what works well and what does not. The results are used to improve future
  offerings of this course and of other courses in the department.
- Evaluations are anonymous, so I cannot see who has completed one. If you do
  not submit something to Canvas through this assignment, I have no way of
  giving you credit.

## Submission and grading

To get credit, do **one** of the following:

- upload a screenshot of your evaluation receipt, or
- type `DONE` in the text entry box for this assignment.

Completing this assignment earns the full points.
