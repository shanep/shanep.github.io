# Course Questions

## About this Discussion

This is an ungraded discussion area that you can use to post general class questions. If you are
unclear on directions, assignments, etc., please feel free to post your question(s) here.

If you know the answer to a question posed by another student, you're invited to help each other
out and post an answer.

[Select the Subscribe button](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Student-Guide/How-do-I-subscribe-to-a-discussion-as-a-student/ta-p/352)
to be notified whenever there's a new post in this discussion.

> ### When to Message Me
>
> If your question is related to a grade you earned or another personal matter, **do not post it
> here**. Instead, contact me directly.
