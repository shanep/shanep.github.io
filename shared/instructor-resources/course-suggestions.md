# Course Suggestions Document

The **Course Suggestions Document** is your roadmap for continuous course improvement. Teaching is
an ongoing learning experience, and this document is here to help you keep track of your notes and
ideas for improving the course so you can easily reference them when preparing for the next
semester. Use this space to jot down notes and suggestions as they arise.

Examples of possible adjustments and improvements might include:

- Clarifying an assignment
- Refining course navigation
- Enhancing student engagement

As you complete the document, if you would like help or advice, consider contacting:

- Your department chair
- [The Help Desk](https://www.boisestate.edu/oit/assistance/) for technical support
- The [eCampus Center Consultation Request Form](https://www.boisestate.edu/ecampus-center/consultations/)

> ### Next Steps
>
> Before making a new copy of your course, review the updates you've listed here to ensure your
> changes are intentional and effective. Once you've applied your updates to the next course
> version, simply delete or cross out past notes to start fresh.
>
> To maintain a clear record of the student experience, we recommend **not modifying an existing
> course** after it has been taught.
