# Instructor Guide: Start Here for Course Setup

The **Instructor Guide** is designed to help you efficiently prepare, manage, and deliver your
online course. Whether you are setting up your course before launch, keeping track of weekly
tasks, or managing grading and student communication, this resource provides step-by-step
instructions and helpful tools.

Use this guide as a reference throughout the term to stay organized and make the most of your
online teaching experience. Inside, you'll find:

- Checklists
- Grading support
- Sample announcements
- Additional resources to ensure a smooth and engaging experience for both you and your students

As courses typically open to students the Friday before the first day of classes, we encourage you
to review the **Course Setup and Launch** section in advance to ensure a seamless start.
