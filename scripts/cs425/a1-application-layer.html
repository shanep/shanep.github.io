Now watch the TTL

Run dig +noall +answer boisestate.edu four more times. Then run dig +noall +answer example.com, wait three seconds, run it again.

TTL for boisestate.eduTTL for example.com, twice

One of them never moves and the other does, sometimes upward. Explain both. You need two facts: a TTL counts down while an answer sits in a cache, and /etc/resolv.conf here lists two resolvers.

The two that fail, and why that is the interesting part

@1.1.1.1 times out; +trace stops after one step. Neither is broken software: both need to send DNS queries directly to a server out on the internet, and this network does not allow it. What do the two failures have in common, and what is sitting between Onyx and the internet that causes both?
