# Getting Started Module Overview

I know you're excited to get going, but before we dive into Module 1 and begin our coursework, there
are some housekeeping details and a few tasks to complete in this Getting Started Module. This
module will likely take you about 1-2 hours, depending on your online course experience and comfort
with technology. The module is designed to do three things:

1. Show you how we're using Canvas in this course.
2. Introduce you to me, the course and your peers.
3. Familiarize you with some of the resources that will make your learning journey easier and more
    rewarding.

material in this module and participating in the Introduction assignment, then jump into Module 1 .
