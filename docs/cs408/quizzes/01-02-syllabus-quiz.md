# 01.02 Syllabus Quiz (15 - 30 min)

**Week 1 · 4 points**

## Overview
Please read the syllabus that is linked in canvas and then answers the questions below!

## Quiz Details
- The quiz is open book. Allowable sources include the textbook, lecture slides, online videos,
  interactive modules, and your notes.
- You may not consult with another person while doing the quiz.
- Question styles may include multiple choice, true or false, and fill-in-the-blanks.
- You only get three attempts at this quiz with your score averaged across all attempts
- You will see your responses and the correct answers after each attempt.
