# 02.03 CS208 Review - Practice Quiz (5 - 6 hrs)

**Week 2 · 50 points**

## Overview

This is a **Practice Quiz** that covers most of the material taught in CS208. This quiz DOES NOT
COUNT toward your final grade! The purpose of this quiz is to allow you to test yourself to see if
you still remember the material that was taught in CS208. Do not worry if you see questions that
you don't know the answer to; it does not mean that you are behind or that you missed something in
CS208. Web development is a very broad field, so there will always be new things to know. During
your quiz, take note of any questions that you don't know the answer to and research them on the
internet. Again, DON'T PANIC if you don't know the answers. Take a deep breath and remember that I
have designed this course for you to succeed, and it is normal not to get 100% on everything :)

## Quiz Details

- The quiz is open-book. Allowable sources include lecture slides, online videos,
  interactive modules, your notes, and the internet.
- Question styles may include multiple choice, true or false, and fill-in-the-blank.
- You can take this quiz as many times as you wish.
- You will see your responses and the correct answers after each attempt.

## Questions

The questions are drawn from a question bank in Canvas, and only there.
