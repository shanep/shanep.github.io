# 01.03 Class Introductions (30 min - 1 hr)

**Week 1 · 10 points**

Over the next few weeks we're going to be working together. Peers offer us support, constructive
criticism, tech advice, tips and tricks, and a little humor just when we need it most!

## Initial Post Directions

So we can get to know one another, please tell us a little bit about yourself and why you're taking
this class.

Click Reply and enter your response to the prompts below. Your post may include:

- A brief introduction to who you are, including at least one thing that might help others remember
  you.
- Why you are taking the class.
- What you hope to learn from the class.

You may add other information you would like to share with your classmates.

Optional: Add a photo.

- Adding a photo to your post: You may include a picture or an image that represents you in your
  post, but it is not required. You can follow these instructions on [embedding images in Canvas
  discussion
  boards](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Student-Guide/How-do-I-embed-an-image-in-a-discussion-reply-as-a-student/ta-p/313).
   If you need assistance with any of these steps, refer to the [Course Resources
  Module](https://boisestatecanvas.instructure.com/courses/48195/modules/635575) or contact the
  Boise State Help Desk (208-426-4357 or <helpdesk@boisestate.edu>).
- Changing your profile picture: You can change your [profile picture in
  Canvas](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Student-Guide/How-do-I-add-a-profile-picture-in-my-user-account-as-a-student/ta-p/518),
  which appears next to your contributions in class and group discussions. Loading a profile photo
  is optional but may help others put a face to your name.
