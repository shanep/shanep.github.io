# 01.05 Personalize Your Canvas Profile (15 min)

**Week 1 · 5 points · due Fri Aug 28, 11:59 PM**

## Objective

This assignment aims to help students familiarize themselves with the features of the Canvas and
create a more engaging, personalized profile that fosters better connections within the learning
community.

### Instructions

1. **Add a Profile Picture**
    1. Upload a clear, appropriate photo **or** avatar to Canvas that reflects your personality or
        interests.
    2. For instructions on how to do this, please review: "[How Do I Add a Profile Picture In My
        User
        Account?](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Canvas-Basics-Guide/How-do-I-add-a-profile-picture-in-my-user-account/ta-p/615332)"
2. [](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Canvas-Basics-Guide/How-do-I-add-a-profile-picture-in-my-user-account/ta-p/615332)**Check
    your display name**
    1. Ensure your name is accurate and professional. You may include a preferred nickname.
    2. For instructions on how to update your display name, please review: "[Updating my displayed
        name in
        Canvas"](https://community.canvaslms.com/t5/Troubleshooting/Updating-my-displayed-name-in-Canvas/ta-p/853)\
