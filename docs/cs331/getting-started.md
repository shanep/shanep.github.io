# Getting Started Overview

**January 11-17 · About 1-2 hours, before Module 1**

I know you're excited to get going, but before we dive into the course content there are some
introductory activities to complete. They will take about one to two hours, depending on your
online course experience and comfort with technology, and they are designed to familiarise you
with the resources that will make the rest of the semester easier.

Start with the [syllabus](index.md). Its Course Workflow section explains the rhythm every week
follows, and nothing in this course spans more than one week.
