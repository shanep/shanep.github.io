## Research

My primary research interests are empirical software engineering, cybersecurity, and
machine learning. I have several publications in high-quality (A) conferences and
journals with collaborators in the Computer Science department at Boise State and
external universities. I have submitted several grants as Co-Principal Investigator
(Co-PI) and have a total of $645,890 in awards directly attributable to my efforts.
